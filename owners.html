How ExitRadar works

buyer intelligence
01 / Detect

Find transition signals.

Look for public business signals such as long operating history, owner-operated positioning, succession language, leadership transitions, dated websites, reduced operating hours, or businesses that have never appeared on an acquisition marketplace.

02 / Score

Rank the ones worth calling.

Combine business quality, local fit, transition signals, digital gaps, and likely owner willingness into a transparent score. The score is a research priority — not a claim about a person's age or private circumstances.

Important: ExitRadar should not infer or expose a person's age. It uses business-level, publicly observable signals instead.
03 / Approach

Lead with a helpful conversation.

Instead of “Are you old and ready to sell?”, the buyer can offer a confidential conversation about succession, partial sale, management transition, or simply preparing the business for a future exit.

04 / Product

Build the buyer's proprietary pipeline.

Next: saved searches, owner/contact enrichment from lawful public sources, valuation ranges, comparable transactions, outreach tracking, and alerts when a business's public signals change.
